# SmaranQ v0.4.18 — public alpha trial

Alpha software, still in development. Expect bugs and unfinished behavior. Report bugs to the developer, Raj Kumar, at contact@rajsharma.space. Include your Mac model, macOS version, reproduction steps, expected behavior, and actual behavior. Remove private meeting content from screenshots or logs.

## Compatibility and trial

Apple silicon (arm64), macOS 14 or later. Not an Intel/universal build.

Valid from 27 September 2026, 00:00 IST until 16 October 2026, 00:00 IST exclusive: usable through 15 October 2026 IST. After expiry, new recordings and intelligence requests are blocked. You can still stop/save an active recording and review/export existing recordings. Hosted AI uses the provider configured in Settings; provider charges may apply.

## Signing disclaimer

Signed with an Apple Development certificate. Not Developer ID signed and not notarized by Apple. macOS may block the first launch because it cannot verify the developer or check the app for malicious software. Another-Mac installation, permission continuity, live recording, native resize/toolbar interactions, VoiceOver, and multidisplay capture have not been fully validated. Public alpha availability does not establish release readiness.

## Install

1. Download the DMG and verify its SHA-256 against the attached checksum file.
2. Stop/save active recordings and quit an older SmaranQ installation. Open the DMG and drag SmaranQ.app to Applications. Eject the DMG, then open the app from Applications.
3. If macOS blocks it and you trust the download, dismiss the warning, open Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway for SmaranQ. Confirm Open and authenticate if asked. The exception is available for about an hour after a blocked launch; retry the launch if needed. Managed Macs may restrict this option.
4. Allow Screen & System Audio Recording (or Screen Recording), Microphone, and optional Camera only for sources you need. Restart the app if macOS prompts you.
5. Confirm v0.4.18 and trial dates in Settings → About SmaranQ. Rehearse a short recording, playback, and export before a meeting.

Only override the first-launch warning for a trusted download. Do not bypass a warning that the app will damage your computer. [Apple: Open apps safely](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).
